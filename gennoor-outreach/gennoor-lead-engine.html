The loop — simple on purpose
1

Find. Ask Claude to search leads by keyword — it uses Vibe Prospecting (company + contact data), Tavily web research, and your own tracker & lead pool. New leads land in the pool.

2

Draft. Claude writes the outreach email from your Outreach Kit templates — under 120 words, one proof link, free session first — as a branded HTML email with the Capability One-Pager attached.

3

Approve. Nothing sends without you. You review each draft in chat and say yes, edit, or skip.

4

Send. Approved emails go out through Outlook on your PC from your own address.

5

Track. Claude checks your inbox through the Microsoft 365 connector; replies appear in the Conversations tab as full threads with their attachments.

6

Log. Every evening at 6:00 PM IST, a clean day-wise Word log is written to Gennoor Work › Logs: searches run, leads added, emails sent, replies received.

Working rules (from your kit)

Lead with the free session · one proof link, not five · under 120 words · one follow-up after 5–7 days, then move on · cap free sessions at ~2/month · start with the SMALL rows marked START HERE.
