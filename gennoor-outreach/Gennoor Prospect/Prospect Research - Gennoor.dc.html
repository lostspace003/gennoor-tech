Where Gennoor's next engagement comes from

Gennoor Tech sells the full AI engagement — diagnose → train → POC → develop → roadmap — to three kinds of buyer: corporates, universities, and government. The ESI and EY batches are the company's delivery record, not its ceiling. This brief maps who to write to across the Gulf, Tanzania and India — and every lead is worked the same way: relationship first, free webinar, free online cohort, then paid and offline.

Sequenced by scale, deliberately: small first, then medium. Small organizations sign on one director's yes — days, not months. Medium ones take a procurement step. The large names (EY, the Tanzanian banks, government) stay on the list as anchors: worked patiently on the ladder in the background, never the first bet.

The Gennoor motion — every lead below is worked on this ladder
1 · Relate
Personal note to a named decision-maker, one proof asset attached. Offer a free 60-min webinar for their team, students or clients — live demos, no pitch.
2 · Free cohort
Convert interest into a free online cohort session — one real module, hands-on. Capture the attendee list, qualify the sponsor.
3 · Paid online
Paid virtual batches, AI-readiness assessment, or a scoped POC sprint — purchase-order money, fast to sign.
4 · Offline + build
On-site weeks (Gulf fly-in, Dar es Salaam), then development and AI-roadmap retainer — the full company engagement.
Guardrail for a lean company: max ~2 free sessions a month, and only when a decision-maker attends or 20+ seats fill. Free is marketing spend — qualify before giving it.
Three buyers, one ladder
Corporates
Small training institutes and MSPs buy delivery capacity on a director's yes; medium firms buy outcomes with one procurement step. Webinar for their team → paid batch → POC of one workflow → build. Decision-maker: owner/L&D head, delivery head, CIO.
Cycle: 2 wks – 2 mo · Lanes A & B
Universities
Guest masterclass (free, high volume, zero friction) → certificate cohort with the department → paid bootcamps, faculty development programs, curriculum licensing of the AB-100 / AI-102 assets. Decision-maker: HoD, dean, training & placement officer.
Cycle: 1 – 3 mo · Lane B & watchlist
Government
Never cold — enter as the AI-delivery arm of already-empaneled partners, and register on tender portals so RFPs find you. National skilling drives (Saudi's SAMAI, Tanzania's digital push) buy exactly what Gennoor teaches. Decision-maker: program office via prime contractor.
Cycle: 3 – 9 mo · Lanes B & C

Lane C — Gulf · corporates + government skilling · position now

The Saudi skilling wave

Saudi Arabia's Cabinet has designated 2026 the Year of Artificial Intelligence, with SDAIA leading a national program spanning government, business and education. Microsoft's commitment to skill 3 million more people by 2030 — delivered with ministries and universities — means official-courseware demand will exceed the local MCT bench for years.

Gennoor's realistic entry is not a Saudi entity — it's being the delivery bench behind the small and medium brands that hold those contracts: the Dubai institutes above, Spectrum's Riyadh operation, Kaplan MENA's KSA programs, Edoxi's government clients. Every Lane-A win compounds here. For visibility, run one free monthly public webinar in Gulf-friendly hours ("Agentic AI for the enterprise — live build") and let partners co-brand it.

Being honest with ourselves
The CRDB tender window has closed. It's cited as proof of live procurement, not an open door — the move is vendor registration and relationships before the next RFP.
Some claims are self-reported (Reviesoft's "1,000+ businesses", institute rankings). Directional evidence of reach, not audited fact.
Rate pressure is real in Lane A. Sub-delivery pays less than direct — its value is cash flow plus meeting end clients. Protect the right to co-brand webinars in every contract.
One company, lean team — sequence, don't sprawl. Small closes fund the pipeline; medium builds the brand; large anchors (EY, banks, government) mature on the ladder behind them. Universities ride alongside as the legitimacy engine.
