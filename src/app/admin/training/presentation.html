🛡️ Objection Handling Guide

Objection Response Strategy
"Too expensive" Focus on ROI: "Our average client sees 3-6 month payback. Let's calculate your potential savings..."
"We're not ready for AI" "That's exactly why we start with a POC - to prove readiness and value with minimal risk"
"Already have IT team" "We complement your IT team with specialized AI expertise they can leverage"
"Need to think about it" "What specific concerns can I address? Let's schedule a follow-up to discuss after your review"
"Bad past experience" "I understand. That's why we start with a POC - you see results before major commitment"
"How are you different?" "We focus on outcomes, not just technology. Our 95% success rate comes from our proven methodology"

Remember: Never argue or get defensive. Acknowledge, empathize, then redirect to value.

⭐ Best Practices & Tips

Do's ✅

  • Research before every outreach
  • Personalize every communication
  • Follow up consistently (5-7 touches)
  • Document all interactions in CRM
  • Share success stories and case studies
  • Always CC admin@gennoor.com
