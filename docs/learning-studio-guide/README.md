# Learning Studio: how to use it

A step-by-step guide to the Gennoor Learning Studio, from the first click to your first lesson. Every picture is a frame of the walkthrough video (`Learning Studio - Walkthrough (4K).mp4`), with the part to look at marked in pink.

## Part 1. Find a course (no sign-in needed)

### 1. Open the menu
Go to gennoor.com. Use the menu button at the top left. Click **Learning Studio**, then **Courses**.

![Menu](01-menu.jpg)

### 2. The Learning Studio
Two parts, side by side: **Certifications & Projects** (hands-on courses on video) and **AI Essentials** (short interactive courses you can finish in about an hour). At the top right you find **Courses**, **Community** and **Sign in**.

![Studio](02-studio.jpg)

### 3. Filter the courses
Scroll to **Available Courses**. Filter by company, type (for example Certification prep) and level. The number next to each filter shows how many courses match. Click **All** to clear a filter.

![Filters](03-filters.jpg)

### 4. Watch a preview
Every card has **Watch preview**. The short video plays right in the card. Use the full-screen button to make it big, and **Close preview** to put the picture back.

![Preview](04-preview.jpg)

### 5. Read the course cards
Each card shows the type, level, number of lessons and hours of video. Open the one that fits you.

![Cards](05-course-cards.jpg)

### 6. Open a course
The course page shows the promo video, the numbers (modules, lessons, quizzes, hours), one stripe for every lesson, and what you will be able to do.

![Course page](06-course-page.jpg)

### 7. The curriculum
Click a module to open its lessons: lectures, labs, demos and quizzes, in learning order.

![Curriculum](07-curriculum.jpg)

### 8. Enroll for free
Click **Enroll free**. Sign in once with Microsoft, a one-time code sent to your email, or a password. Your progress is saved on every device.

![Enroll](08-enroll.jpg)

### 9. Ask the chat
The round button at the bottom right opens the site chat. Type a question and press Enter. Answers come with links. You can also ask for a callback from the team.

![Chat](09-chat.jpg)

### 10. Search the site
Open the menu and click **Search**. Type a word to find pages and guides.

![Search](10-search.jpg)

### 11. AI Essentials
Free interactive courses with animated slides, narration and a short quiz.

![AI Essentials](11-ai-essentials.jpg)

### 12. Community
Anyone can read the community. Sign in to post, reply, like and vote.

![Community](12-community.jpg)

### 13. Custom training
Not finding what you need? Click **Custom training** at the top right, fill in the form and send it. The team writes back with a plan.

![Custom training](13-custom-training.jpg)

## Part 2. Learn (after you sign in and enroll)

### 14. The learning page
**Start the course** opens the player. Under the video: play, 10 seconds back, 10 seconds forward, volume, speed, captions and full screen. Space plays or pauses, arrow keys skip 10 seconds, F opens full screen. **Mark as complete** records your progress, **Next lesson** moves on.

![Learning page](14-learning-page.jpg)

### 15. Ask about this course
The **Ask** tab on the left edge opens a chat about the current course. It answers course questions only. For lab problems and code, use the discussion.

![Ask](15-ask-about-course.jpg)

### 16. Course content
Switch to **Course content** on the right to see your progress and every module. Click any lesson to jump to it.

![Course content](16-course-content.jpg)

### 17. Take a quiz
Quizzes sit between lessons. Pick one answer per question, then **Submit answers**. The pass mark is shown at the top.

![Quiz](17-quiz.jpg)

### 18. Course discussion
**Discussion**, at the top right, is the place for questions and answers. **Start a topic** opens a rich text editor: bold, italic, heading, lists, quote and link.

![Discussion](18-discussion-editor.jpg)

### 19. Polls
**Create a poll**: write the question, add two or more choices and post. Everyone can vote, and results show as percentages. You can change your vote.

![Poll](19-poll.jpg)

### 20. Community, signed in
The community works the same way for everyone in the Learning Studio.

![Community signed in](20-community-signed-in.jpg)

## For the Gennoor team: admin

In **Admin > Community** the team can:

- **Publish** announcements, polls and quizzes in the community or in a course (the quiz marks the right answer and shows an explanation after learners answer);
- see poll and quiz results, and who answered what;
- reply as the team, close or reopen a poll, hide or delete any post or reply;
- moderate course reviews.
