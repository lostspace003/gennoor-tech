Question 1
A knowledge worker has asked Claude to produce a written procedure that the team will reference across multiple review cycles, and update it when the underlying process changes. Which output format is most appropriate?
A. Generate the procedure as the chat response and copy the text into the team's document store after each review.
B. Generate the procedure as a series of numbered steps in the chat and keep the chat itself as the reference for future edits.
C. Generate the procedure as an Artifact, which the team can open, edit, and save as a stable reference for ongoing use.
D. Generate the procedure as the chat response and save the entire chat transcript to the team's document store as the working reference.

Explanation: In Claude, an Artifact renders a standalone document in a dedicated side panel that team members can view, edit, version, and save as a stable reference across review cycles, rather than burying the procedure inside a scrolling chat transcript.
