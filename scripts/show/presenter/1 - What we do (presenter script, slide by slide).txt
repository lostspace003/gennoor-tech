GENNOOR TECH: WHAT WE DO
The presenter's script, slide by slide

Goes with:   1 - What we do (presentation for client calls).pptx
Same story:  1 - What we do (website and LinkedIn, wide 16x9).mp4

This script is for anyone who presents Gennoor Tech to a client. You do not need a
technical background. For each of the 15 slides it gives you:

   THE SLIDE SHOWS   what the client is looking at
   SAY               words you can say as they are
   IN PLAIN WORDS    what it means, so you can answer in your own words

The whole presentation takes about 12 to 15 minutes. A 5-minute version is at the end.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE THE CALL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Open the presentation, press F5, and share your screen.
- The parts of a slide come one at a time. Keep pressing the SPACE bar until the slide
  is complete (most slides end with a blue button at the bottom), then say your lines.
- The blue button opens that page of our website. Use it only if the client asks to see
  more.
- Do not quote a price. Say: "The cost depends on the size of the team and what we build.
  Once we understand your need, we send it in writing."
- Do not name other clients.
- If you do not know an answer, say: "Let me note it and come back to you today."

SLIDE 1     OUR VISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SLIDE SHOWS
   Our vision, in four short lines.

SAY
   "Let me start with why we exist. Our vision is a world where anyone can learn the
   skills that work asks for, and prove them. That is what we are building."

IN PLAIN WORDS
   We help people learn skills they can use in their job, and show proof that they have
   learned them: a quiz passed, a project finished, a certificate.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2     WHY WE EXIST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SLIDE SHOWS
   "Most training ends when the slides end. It should end when you can do the work."
   And four words: Watch, Practise, Build, Prove.

SAY
   "Most training ends when the slides end. People watch, nod, and go back to their desk
   unable to do the thing. We think training should end when you can do the work. So our
   learners watch, practise, build something, and then prove it."

IN PLAIN WORDS
   The problem we solve: people sit through training and still cannot do the job.
   Our answer: every course makes the learner do the work, not only watch it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3     INSIDE EVERY COURSE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SLIDE SHOWS
   On the left, one of our courses as a learner sees it. On the right, the five things
   every course has: short video lessons, labs you run yourself, an assistant in the
   lesson, a quiz in every module, a certificate at the end.

SAY
   "Every course of ours is built the same way. Short video lessons, a few minutes each,
   with captions. Labs, where the learner does the exercise on their own computer with a
   guide for every step. An assistant inside the lesson, which answers questions from
   the course itself. A quiz in every module, so they know they have understood. And a
   certificate at the end."

IN PLAIN WORDS
   A "module" is a chapter. A "lab" is a practical exercise. The "assistant" is a chat
   box inside the lesson. The course in the picture is one of our own, AI-103.

BLUE BUTTON   opens a real course page, where preview lessons play without signing in.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 4     ASK ANYTHING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SLIDE SHOWS
   A learner asking the assistant a question, and the assistant's answer. Beside it:
   the assistant is inside every lesson, every course has a discussion page, and every
   lesson has captions.

SAY
   "When a learner is stuck, they should not have to wait for the next class. They type

IN PLAIN WORDS
   The assistant is an AI helper that has read the course, so its answer fits what the
   learner is studying. The question shown on the slide is only an example from a

BLUE BUTTON   opens the same course page.

   A lab guide (a list of steps), a black window where the learner types on their own

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SLIDE SHOWS
   Three steps: we learn your work, we build the course, we teach it live. Then what we
   cover: Claude, Microsoft AI, automation, Microsoft official courses, exam
   preparation, custom courses.

SAY
   "For a team, we go further than a ready-made course. First we learn your work: what

IN PLAIN WORDS
   This is the slide most companies care about. The message: training made for their
   team, using their own kind of work as the examples. A good question to ask here:
   "What does your team spend most of its time on?"

SLIDE 9     CUSTOM AUTOMATION TRAINING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SLIDE SHOWS
   One weekly job done by hand (requests arrive, sort them, draft the reply, update the
   sheet), and the same job after the course: "Now it runs when they ask. A person
   checks." Below, where teams usually start.

SAY
   "We also teach automation, and it is a custom course every time. Think of one job
   your team does by hand every week. We choose that one process with you. We build the
   course on it, with your own examples and files. And your people automate it
   themselves, during the course. At the end, that job runs when they ask for it, and a
   person still checks the result. Teams usually start with sorting documents and
   emails, routine questions, reports, or moving data from one tool to another."

IN PLAIN WORDS
   "Automation" means the computer does the repeated steps of a job. We do not take the
   job away from the team. We teach the team to automate it, so they can do the next one
   without us.

BLUE BUTTON   opens our training page.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SLIDE SHOWS
   A learning website with "YOUR LOGO" at the top, and three lines: Your courses. Your

BLUE BUTTON   opens our contact page.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 11    AFTER THE TRAINING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SLIDE SHOWS
   On the left: a usual course takes 3 to 5 days in a classroom; ours aims at about 60%
   less classroom time. On the right: a "doubt desk", where a learner asks a question a
   week later and a trainer answers.

SAY
   "Two things clients always ask about: time, and what happens afterwards. Time: a
   usual course keeps people in a classroom for three to five days. With us, people
   learn the ideas from short lessons and practise in labs first, so the trainer's time
   is kept for the hard parts. Our aim is about sixty percent less classroom time. How
   much exactly depends on the course and the team. And afterwards: the real questions
   come a week later, back at the desk. So there is a doubt desk. A trainer answers, the
   answer is kept for the next person, and the course stays open."

IN PLAIN WORDS
   Sixty percent is an AIM, not a promise. Always say "our aim" and "it depends on the
   course and the team". A "doubt desk" is a place where learners ask questions after
   the training.

BLUE BUTTON   opens our contact page.

   bigger commitment. "Scope" means exactly what will be built and what will not. The

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUESTIONS CLIENTS ASK, AND A PLAIN ANSWER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"How much does it cost?"
   "It depends on the size of the team and what we build for you. Tell me a little about
   your need, and we will send the cost in writing."

"How long does a training take?"
   "It depends on the course and the team. People learn from short lessons and labs
   first, so the classroom time is shorter than usual."

"Is it online or at our office?"
   "Both. Live online, or at your office."

"Do our people need to know coding?"
   "It depends on the course. Many of our courses are for people who do not code. The AI
   Weekend and the AI Essentials courses need no coding."

"Will AI replace the person doing the job?"
   "In what we build, a person stays in charge and checks the result. We automate the
   repeated steps."

"Can we see it before we decide?"
   "Yes. You can watch a preview lesson now, without signing up. And for automation we
   can build a small working prototype on your own examples first."

"Who teaches?"
   "Our founder, Jalal Ahmed Khan, is a Microsoft Certified Trainer. He trains corporate
   teams on AI and cloud, and builds our courses."

Anything else: "Let me note it and come back to you today."
