## 6. Do's and Don'ts (from the Certified kit)

**Do**
- Wait for your Certified badge email before posting.
- Use the approved tier line and the boilerplate.
- Tag Anthropic/Claude so they can amplify you.

**Don't**
- ❌ Say "Anthropic partner", "official Anthropic partner", "Anthropic-certified" or "certified by Anthropic".
- ❌ Call yourself Select, Preferred or Global Premier.
- ❌ Say you are listed in the Claude Partner Directory (that starts at Select).
- ❌ Mention tier requirements, deal economics, or incentive amounts.
- ❌ Claim ranking or superiority ("the only", "the top", "leading Claude partner").
- ❌ Name a customer without written consent.
- ❌ Issue a press release or newswire announcement about Certified status.
- ❌ Alter, recolor or redraw the badge.

**Phrases to avoid → use instead**

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use |
|---|---|
| "Official Anthropic Partner" | "Certified in the Claude Partner Network" |
| "Anthropic-certified firm" | "Gennoor is Certified in the Claude Partner Network" |
| "Leading/top Claude partner in India" | (drop the ranking) "Certified in the Claude Partner Network" |
| "Certified by Anthropic" | "Our consultants hold Claude certifications" |
